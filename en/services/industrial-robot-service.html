Industrial robot service

Bydgoszcz, northern Poland and all of Poland

We service robots and manipulators working with injection moulding machines: from diagnosing and repairing axis mechanics, through drives and controls, to programming and EOAT grippers.

Call: +48 733 590 940 Send an enquiry

Scope of robot service

We look for the cause of the fault, not just the symptom, so that the machine gets back to work and does not stop again. We take measurements at the machine, and we can make missing parts ourselves.

  • Diagnosis and repair of X, Y and Z axis mechanics
  • Replacement of toothed belts, rollers, bearings and linear guides
  • Belt tension setting with a professional Optibelt tension meter
  • Replacement of damaged profiles and structural parts
  • Drives, servo drives, controls and communication
  • Robot programming and cycle optimisation
  • Gripper (EOAT) design and manufacture
