Injection moulding machine service

Bydgoszcz, northern Poland and all of Poland

We service and repair injection moulding machines in plastics processing plants: from fault diagnosis, through mechanical, hydraulic and electrical repairs, to getting production running again.

Call: +48 733 590 940 Send an enquiry

Scope of injection moulding machine service

We look for the cause of the fault, not just the symptom, so that the machine gets back to work and does not stop again. We take measurements at the machine, and we can make missing parts ourselves.

  • Diagnosis of mechanical, hydraulic and electrical faults
  • Seal replacement on clamping, rapid traverse and injection unit cylinders
  • Clamping unit repairs, including locks and tie bars
  • Electronics, power supply and CAN bus diagnostics with an oscilloscope at the machine
  • Controls, drives and servo drives
  • Relocation and recommissioning of injection moulding machines
  • Spare parts made on demand by CNC machining and 3D printing

Engel injection moulding machine service

We service Engel injection moulding machines, including tie-bar-less models. We replace the seals of clamping and rapid traverse cylinders and diagnose hydraulics and electrics. During one repair we found a bolt inside a clamping cylinder that was destroying it.

  • Projects:
  • Clamping cylinder seals on a tie-bar-less Engel injection moulding machine
  • Rapid traverse cylinder seals on an Engel injection moulding machine

KraussMaffei injection moulding machine service

We repair KraussMaffei injection moulding machines, including large two-platen MX series machines. Among other jobs, we carried out a priority Saturday repair of the locks on a KraussMaffei 2700MX.

  • Projects:
  • Repair of the locks on a KraussMaffei 2700MX machine

Demag injection moulding machine service

We service Demag (Sumitomo Demag) injection moulding machines: fault diagnosis, hydraulics, seals, electrics and controls.

Stork injection moulding machine service

We service and repair Stork injection moulding machines, including older machines, where fast diagnosis and making replacement parts are crucial.

JSW injection moulding machine service

We service JSW (Japan Steel Works) injection moulding machines and the robots and auxiliary equipment that work with them.

Toyo injection moulding machine service

We diagnose and repair Toyo injection moulding machines: mechanics, drives, controls and power systems.

Arburg injection moulding machine service

We replace the seals of the clamping unit and the injection unit on Arburg injection moulding machines, for example for a manufacturer of reflectors for the automotive industry.

  • Projects:
  • Seal replacement on an Arburg injection moulding machine

Battenfeld injection moulding machine service

We service Battenfeld injection moulding machines: diagnostics, hydraulics, seals, electrics and controls.

Service area
