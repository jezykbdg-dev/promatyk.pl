Repair of an injection mould turnover machine

Diagnostics and repair for a subcontractor of the Volkswagen Group, Tesla and Toyota, among others.

Scope of work

We diagnosed and repaired an injection mould turnover machine for an automotive subcontractor supplying components to the Volkswagen Group, Tesla and Toyota, among others. A working turnover machine allows heavy moulds to be rotated safely during maintenance and changeovers, which in automotive production translates directly into continuity of supply.
