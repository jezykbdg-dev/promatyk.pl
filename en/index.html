What we do

Complete care of machines and robots in plastics processing plants: from the first diagnosis to commissioning, plus parts made in our own workshop.

Diagnostics and repair

We diagnose and repair injection moulding machines, industrial robots and auxiliary equipment. We look for the cause, not just the symptom, so that production gets back up and running and does not stop again.

  • Mechanical and hydraulic faults
  • Electrics, controls, drives and servo drives
  • Auxiliary equipment and feeders

R&D and EOAT

We optimise production, write programs and design and build grippers (EOAT) to the customer’s specifications. Solutions tailored to the process and the product.

  • Cycle and process optimisation
  • Robot and controller programming
  • Gripper (EOAT) design and manufacture
Ask about this service Robot service: more

Dryers and sieves

We service plastic dryers and supply original molecular sieves. We make sure granulate drying is stable and the material is properly regenerated, without production stoppages or wasted raw material.

  • Dryer servicing and repair
  • Sale of original molecular sieves
  • Regeneration and a stable drying process

CAD, 3D printing and CNC

We design in CAD and then make the part in-house: we 3D print it from engineering plastics or CNC mill it. From idea to finished part.

  • CAD design and 3D model
  • 3D printing from engineering plastics
  • CNC milling of parts and fixtures

Relocation and commissioning

We help with the relocation and recommissioning of injection moulding machines and robots: from disassembly and securing the machine, through transport and installation, to the first production start-up.

  • Machine disassembly and securing
  • Transport, installation and levelling
  • Commissioning and operator training

Why Promatyk

We do not replace parts by trial and error. We measure, find the cause and get the machine working again as fast as possible.

We look for the cause, not the symptom

While replacing the seals of a clamping cylinder, we found a bolt inside that was destroying the cylinder. Replacing the seals alone would not have solved anything.

How we work

A simple, predictable process: from your first call to a running machine or a finished part.

  1. 01

    Contact and problem description

    You call or write. We gather information about the machine, the symptoms and the operating conditions.

  2. 02

    Diagnosis and quote

    We diagnose the fault on site or remotely and present the scope of work and the cost.

  3. 03

    Repair and delivery

    We carry out the repair, relocation, commissioning, R&D work, or the design and 3D printing / CNC milling.

  4. 04

    Start-up and support

    We bring the machine back into production, hand over the finished parts and stay in touch.
